Profile

AI Archaea is a curator-led initiative for organizing knowledge, tools, and public communication around archaeal biology. The work connects domain questions with transparent computational practice: sources remain visible, versions are recorded, and AI-generated outputs are treated as drafts that require human evaluation.

The project is developed openly through GitHub. It is designed as a practical research interface rather than a claim of automated expertise.

Research Interests

Evolution
Archaeal diversity and deep history. Phylogeny, taxonomy, genome evolution, and the changing boundaries of known archaeal lineages.
Ecology
Archaea in environmental systems. Distribution, adaptation, and community roles across oceans, sediments, soils, and extreme habitats.
Metabolism
Energy and biogeochemical cycling. Methanogenesis, methane oxidation, ammonia oxidation, carbon fixation, and metabolic innovation.
Interactions
Hosts, partners, and viruses. Symbiosis, syntrophy, virus-host dynamics, and ecological networks involving archaea.
Methods
Research intelligence. Literature synthesis, evidence graphs, reproducible analysis, and careful use of language models in scientific work.

Current work and project criteria

Priority is given to projects that make research questions easier to inspect, reproduce, or communicate. A useful project should expose its sources, document its transformations, and remain understandable without depending on a single model or platform.

Methods and Workflows

01
Question before tool. Start with a biological or information problem, then select the smallest adequate method.
02
Evidence before fluency. Preserve citations, retrieval dates, identifiers, versions, and uncertainty.
03
Reproducibility before scale. Prefer scripts, explicit parameters, and reviewable intermediate outputs.
04
Human verification. Use AI for acceleration and exploration, not as a substitute for domain judgment.

Writing and Outreach

The English website provides a concise research-facing profile. Longer Chinese-language explanations, research maps, and public science materials are maintained on the dedicated AI古菌中文科普 page.

Editorial principles

Separate evidence from interpretation; explain technical terms without flattening uncertainty; link to primary material whenever possible; and state clearly where AI assistance has been used.

Contact and Collaboration

The most reliable public contact point is GitHub. For a concrete collaboration, please include the research question, available materials, expected timeline, data-access constraints, and the form of contribution you have in mind.
